**Investment Platforms** की पूरी list — Beginners से लेकर advanced investors के लिए, ताकि आसानी से decide किया जा सके कि कौन सा platform किस type के investment के लिए सही है।

---

## **Stock Market Investment Platforms**

| Platform                           | Best For                 | Key Features                                     |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Zerodha Kite**                   | Beginners & Intermediate | Low brokerage, easy UI, charts, research tools   |
| **Upstox**                         | Beginners                | Low cost trading, IPO applications, mutual funds |
| **Groww**                          | Beginners                | Stocks + mutual funds in one app                 |
| **Angel One**                      | All levels               | Advisory, portfolio analysis                     |
| **ICICI Direct / HDFC Securities** | Conservative investors   | Bank integrated, research reports                |

---

## **Mutual Fund Investment Platforms**

| Platform             | Best For             | Key Features                              |
| -------------------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **ET Money**         | Beginners            | Direct mutual funds, goal-based investing |
| **Groww**            | Beginners            | Easy SIP setup, track returns             |
| **Kuvera**           | Tech-savvy investors | Family portfolio tracking, goal planning  |
| **Paytm Money**      | Small investors      | Low minimum SIP amount, simple UI         |
| **MF Utility (MFU)** | Advanced investors   | Access to all AMCs, one-time KYC          |

---

## **Government Schemes & Bonds**

| Platform                | Best For                     | Key Features                 |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **NSDL / Karvy Portal** | NSC, PPF, Sukanya Samriddhi  | Online form submission       |
| **RBI Retail Direct**   | G-Sec & Sovereign Gold Bonds | Direct purchase from RBI     |
| **Post Office Online**  | RD, TD, KVP, MIS             | Traditional safe investments |

---

## **Real Estate Investment**

| Platform           | Best For                         | Key Features                    |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Property Share** | Fractional real estate investing | Commercial property investments |
| **Strata**         | High-value property owners       | Passive rental income           |
| **NoBroker**       | Direct buy/sell without brokers  | Residential investments         |

## **Cryptocurrency Platforms** *(High Risk)*

| Platform             | Best For       | Key Features                    |
| -------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **CoinSwitch Kuber** | Beginners      | Simple UI, multiple coins       |
| **WazirX**           | Traders        | Advanced charts, P2P trading    |

---

💡 **Platform चुनने के Tips**

1. **Low brokerage + Easy UI** → Zerodha, Groww
2. **Goal-based Mutual Funds** → ET Money, Kuvera
3. **Safe Government Schemes** → RBI Retail Direct, Post Office
4. **High Risk–High Return** → Cryptocurrency, Startup Investing
5. **Passive Income** → REITs, Fractional Real Estate Platforms
