## **Life का Financial Roadmap** (Step-by-Step)

## **Stage 1: Foundation Building (Age 20–30)**

🎯 **Primary Focus**: Habits, Skills, और Protection

* **Income Skills Develop** – Main job + side hustle skills
* **Budgeting Habit** – Wants vs Needs की आदत डालना
* **Emergency Fund** – 6 महीने के खर्च का
* **Insurance** – Term Plan + Health Plan
* **Debt Avoidance** – Credit card trap से दूर रहना
* **First Investment** – SIP in Index Funds / PPF

---

## **Stage 2: Growth Phase (Age 30–40)**

🎯 **Primary Focus**: Family Security + Wealth Creation

* **Income Growth** – Promotions, Business Expansion, Side Income
* **Home & Family Protection** – Bigger term insurance, family health cover
* **Goal-based Investments** – बच्चों की पढ़ाई, Home loan prepayment
* **Asset Diversification** – Equity + Debt + Gold + Real Estate
* **Tax Planning** – Section 80C, 80D, HRA benefits
* **Retirement Fund Start** – NPS / EPF + Equity MFs

---

## **Stage 3: Acceleration Phase (Age 40–50)**

🎯 **Primary Focus**: Asset Growth + Debt Clearance

* **High Investment Rate** – Income का 30–40% invest करना
* **Debt-Free Target** – Home loan closure
* **Wealth Protection** – Health top-up, critical illness insurance
* **Passive Income Build** – Rental property, dividend portfolio, REITs
* **Portfolio Rebalancing** – Risk कम करना (equity से debt shift gradually)

---

## **Stage 4: Pre-Retirement Phase (Age 50–60)**

🎯 **Primary Focus**: Wealth Safety + Retirement Preparation

* **Retirement Corpus Lock** – Safe investments में shift
* **Passive Income Activation** – SWP from mutual funds, rentals, pensions
* **Estate Planning** – Will, nominations, trust (if needed)
* **Debt-Free Confirmation** – कोई loan नहीं होना चाहिए
* **Healthcare Security** – Big medical cover & emergency corpus

---

## **Stage 5: Retirement & Legacy Phase (Age 60+)**

🎯 **Primary Focus**: Peaceful & Secure Life

* **Low-Risk Portfolio** – FD, Debt Funds, Senior Citizen Savings Scheme, PPF
* **Monthly Income Plan** – SWP, annuities, pension
* **Health Care Fund** – Medical inflation को ध्यान में रखकर
* **Wealth Transfer Plan** – Kids/grandkids के लिए assets का smooth transfer
* **Lifestyle Planning** – Travel, hobbies, philanthropy

---
