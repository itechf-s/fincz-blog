## 🏦 **Savings Account, Current Account, FD और RD Explained**

अगर आप financial literacy सीख रहे हैं तो **bank accounts और deposit types** को समझना बहुत ज़रूरी है।


---

## **Savings Account (बचत खाता)**

**Purpose:**

* Salary या income receive करना
* रोज़मर्रा के transactions के लिए
* थोड़ा interest earn करना

**Key Features:**

* Interest rate \~3-4% (bank पर depend)
* Limited free transactions (withdrawal limit हो सकती है)
* ATM card, cheque book, net banking मिलता है
* Individual और joint दोनों के नाम पर खुल सकता है

**Best For:**

* Salary earners
* Small saving और daily use

---

## **Current Account (करेंट खाता)**

**Purpose:**

* Business transactions
* High volume money transfer

**Key Features:**

* **Interest नहीं मिलता** (0%)
* Unlimited transactions allowed
* High minimum balance required
* Overdraft facility (bank से temporary loan) मिल सकती है

**Best For:**

* Business owners
* Traders / Shopkeepers
* High cash flow accounts

---

## **Fixed Deposit (FD)**

**Purpose:**

* Fixed period के लिए पैसा deposit करके interest कमाना

**Key Features:**

* Tenure: 7 days – 10 years
* Interest rate \~6-8% (bank पर depend)
* Safe investment (Bank + DICGC insured ₹5 lakh तक)
* Premature withdrawal possible but penalty लग सकती है
* Loan ले सकते हैं FD के against (\~90% तक)

**Best For:**

* Low-risk saving
* Short or medium-term goals
* Emergency Fund का हिस्सा (Breakable FD)

---

## **Recurring Deposit (RD)**

**Purpose:**

* हर महीने fixed amount deposit करके saving habit develop करना

**Key Features:**

* Tenure: 6 months – 10 years
* Monthly fixed deposit amount
* Interest rate \~6-7% (FD जैसा)
* Premature closure possible with penalty
* Compounding से decent return

**Best For:**

* Regular savers
* Short-term goals (Travel, gadgets)
* Lump sum invest नहीं कर सकते तो RD best है

---

## **Quick Comparison Table**

| Feature            | Savings Account    | Current Account       | FD                  | RD                        |
| ------------------ | ------------------ | --------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Interest Rate      | 3-4%               | 0%                    | 6-8%                | 6-7%                      |
| Withdrawal Limit   | Limited            | Unlimited             | Lock-in (Breakable) | Lock-in (Monthly deposit) |
| Best For           | Daily use & saving | Business transactions | Lump sum saving     | Regular small saving      |
| Liquidity          | High               | High                  | Medium              | Medium                    |
| Overdraft Facility | Rare               | Yes                   | Loan against FD     | No                        |

---

## ✅ **Bottom Line**

* **Savings Account** → रोज़मर्रा का use + छोटी saving
* **Current Account** → Business और high transactions
* **FD** → Lump sum safe investment
* **RD** → Monthly disciplined saving

> 💡 Practical Tip:
> Emergency Fund का 50% savings account या sweep FD में, और 50% breakable FD या liquid fund में रख सकते हैं।
